Measurement details & limitations

Busy = 100 × Δchannel_busy / Δchannel_active, from valid driver survey counters. Synthetic example: ch6 720/1000 ms = 72%; ch1 280/1000 ms = 28%; ch11 510/1000 ms = 51%. Three comparable 1-second windows per measured channel, same width; final window shown. Candidate observations require driver support and safe sampling.

AP observations are deduplicated BSSID counts and strongest RSSI, not airtime utilization. No SSIDs or BSSIDs exposed here. A WiFi scan is not a spectrum analyzer and cannot promise the least congested channel. Unknown is never zero.
